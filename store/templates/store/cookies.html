    Cookies Policy

    Last updated: 11 September 2026

    What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember who you are and how you're using it between page loads.

    Cookies We Use

    We only use cookies that are essential for the Site to work. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics cookies.

    Cookie Purpose Duration
    sessionid Keeps you signed in and remembers your cart between pages. Ends when your session expires or you log out.
    csrftoken Protects your account and forms from cross-site request forgery attacks. About 1 year, or until cleared.

    Managing Cookies

    Because these cookies are essential to signing in, keeping your cart, and keeping the Site secure, blocking them in your browser will prevent parts of the Site (like checkout) from working correctly. You can still clear or block cookies at any time through your browser's settings.

    Changes to This Policy

    If we ever introduce analytics or advertising cookies, we will update this page first. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
