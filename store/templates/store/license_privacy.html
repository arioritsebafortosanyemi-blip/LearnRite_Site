    License & Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 11 September 2026

    1. About This Policy

    This policy explains how LearnRite International Publishers ("LearnRite", "we", "us") licenses the content on this website and handles personal data collected through learnritepublishers.com (the "Site"), in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 ("NDPA"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

    2. Copyright & License

    All book content, cover artwork, descriptions, logos and text on this Site are the property of LearnRite International Publishers or its contributors and are protected by Nigerian and international copyright law. Our books are sold exclusively through LearnRite directly to verified schools and individual customers - resale, redistribution, or stocking of our titles in bookshops or other outlets without our written permission is prohibited.

    You may browse the Site and view its content for personal, non-commercial use. You may not copy, reproduce, republish, or create derivative works from any book content, images, or text on this Site without our prior written consent.

    3. Information We Collect

    Depending on how you use the Site, we collect:

    • Account information: username, email address, password (stored securely, never in plain text), account type (individual or school), location (Lagos or outside Lagos), and organization name for school accounts.
    • Delivery details: full name, phone number, delivery address, and an optional landmark, used to fulfil and deliver your orders.
    • Order information: books purchased, quantities, prices, order status, and payment-claim confirmations.
    • Reviews and ratings: star ratings you submit on books you've purchased, linked to your username.
    • Wishlist activity: books you've saved for later.
    • Chatbot conversations: messages you send to our AI support assistant, so it can respond to your question.
    • Google account information (name, email, profile picture) if you choose to sign in with Google, provided to us by Google with your consent.

    4. How We Use Your Information

    We use your information to:

    • Create and manage your account, and show you the correct pricing for your account type and location.
    • Process, fulfil, and deliver your orders, and send you delivery updates by phone or email.
    • Verify bank transfer payments and confirm your order.
    • Display your book ratings and manage your wishlist.
    • Respond to questions through our AI chatbot, including looking up your real order status or book pricing when you ask - the chatbot never invents this information, it is always pulled from our records.
    • Prevent fraud, such as an account switching type to obtain incorrect pricing.
    • Send you newsletter updates, if you've subscribed.
    • Comply with our legal obligations.

    5. Who We Share Information With

    We do not sell your personal data. We share limited information with:

    • Google - only if you choose "Sign in with Google", to authenticate your account.
    • Anthropic (our AI provider) - chatbot messages are processed to generate a response to your question.
    • Our hosting provider - to securely store and run the Site and its database.
    • Our staff - to verify payments, process orders, and respond to support requests.

    We do not collect or store your card or bank login details on this Site - orders are paid for by direct bank transfer, arranged between you and your own bank.

    6. Your Rights Under the NDPA

    You have the right to:

    • Access the personal data we hold about you.
    • Request correction of inaccurate information (account type, location, organization name, and email are locked once set to prevent pricing fraud - email us at learninsideout@yahoo.com if any of these need updating).
    • Request deletion of your account and personal data, subject to our need to keep order records for legal and accounting purposes.
    • Object to or restrict certain uses of your data.
    • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
    • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you believe your rights have been violated.

    To exercise any of these rights, email us at learninsideout@yahoo.com.

    7. Data Retention & Security

    We keep your account and order information for as long as your account is active, and afterward for as long as necessary to meet our legal, accounting, and dispute-resolution obligations. Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing, and access to customer data is restricted to authorized staff.

    8. Children's Data

    This Site is intended for use by schools and adult individuals placing orders, not by children directly. School accounts are managed by the institution, not by pupils.

    9. Cookies

    The Site uses a small number of essential cookies. See our Cookies Policy for details.

    10. Changes to This Policy

    We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

    11. Contact Us

    Questions about this policy? Email learninsideout@yahoo.com or call +234-907-776-5490.
